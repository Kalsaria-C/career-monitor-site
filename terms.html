Purpose

Career Monitor is a personal tool maintained by Chintan Kalsaria for monitoring public career listings and sending requested alerts. This website is informational and offers no public account registration or paid service.

Listings and eligibility

Job postings belong to their respective employers. Listings may change or close, and extracted requirements may be incomplete or misinterpreted. Review the original posting before relying on a match, including experience, location, and visa-sponsorship details. The monitor does not guarantee employment, eligibility, sponsorship, completeness, or uninterrupted operation.

Authorized use

Use only accounts and data sources you are authorized to access. The application must respect applicable provider terms, access restrictions, and rate limits. It does not submit applications or contact employers.

Availability and stopping use

Local scheduling depends on the owner's computer, internet connection, permissions, valid Gmail authorization, and source availability. Disable the schedule and revoke Google access to stop use. See the Privacy Policy for retention and deletion details.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project's GitHub issues. Keep credentials and private account information out of public issues.
