Personal career search

Relevant openings.
One daily email.

Career Monitor checks public company career pages, matches openings against the owner's preferences, and sends a digest of newly discovered matches through the owner's authorized Gmail account.

Choose companies

An editable company list keeps the search focused. Supported public feeds and career pages are checked on a daily schedule.

Match preferences

Roles are evaluated against skills, experience, location, work arrangement, and any stated visa-sponsorship requirements.

Receive new matches

Local history prevents repeat alerts. Emails include the role, matching reasons, and a link to the original posting.

Why Gmail access is requested

The desktop application requests gmail.send permission solely to send the requested alerts and setup-test messages. It does not request permission to read, search, or delete inbox messages.

Read the Privacy Policy for data use, storage, retention, and revocation details.

A personal desktop application

Career Monitor is maintained by Chintan Kalsaria for personal use. The monitor runs on the owner's computer; this website provides information about the application and does not run the monitor, collect Gmail credentials, or offer public registration.

It reports openings for review and does not submit job applications. Listings and eligibility must be confirmed on the employer's website.

For support or privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the project's GitHub issues. Do not include credentials or private account information in public issues.
